Delete your Torchless account

Torchless by Brick & Signal LLC

A Torchless account is optional. You only have one if you signed in with Google or Apple to share jar ratings or photos.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Torchless and go to the JARS tab.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

Can't open the app?

Email the developer address on the Torchless page in Google Play from the email you signed in with, and ask us to delete your Torchless account. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in (the link to your Google or Apple account).
  • Your profile: display name, Instagram handle and your 21+ confirmation.
  • Every rating you shared. They drop out of GLOBAL scores straight away.
  • Every photo you shared to the Global shelf, and your likes.

What stays

  • Jar names (producer, strain and type) you added to the shared catalog stay, with no name attached to them, so other people's ratings keep working.
  • Your jars, photos, prices and dab history stay on your phone, since they were never on our server. Delete them in the app, or clear the app's storage.

We keep no backups of deleted accounts.
