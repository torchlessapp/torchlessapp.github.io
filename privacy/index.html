Torchless Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Torchless is made by Brick & Signal LLC. It lets you control a compatible e-rig over Bluetooth and keep a journal of your jars and dabs. This policy covers the Torchless Android app and the Torchless web app at torchlessapp.github.io.

The short version

Torchless has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you log stays on your phone. Signing in is optional. Only if you sign in to share ratings do we keep a small account: your sign-in, a display name, and the jar ratings you choose to share. We never sell or share your data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your jars: strain, producer, type, rating, review, photos, flavors and price details.
  • Your dab history: time, length, temperature, rating, dab size and notes.
  • App settings, such as your look (dark or bright) and that you confirmed you are 21 or older.

These are saved in your browser's storage on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. If you use Save backup, the backup file goes wherever you choose to save it.

If you sign in (optional)

You can sign in with Google or Apple to add your jar ratings to GLOBAL, the average of everyone's ratings. Then we keep, on our server (Supabase, hosted in the United States):

  • Your account ID and the email address Google or Apple gives us (Apple may give a private relay address).
  • The display name you pick, an Instagram handle if you add one, and when you confirmed you are 21 or older.
  • For each jar you share: producer, strain, type and your 0 to 10 rating. Turn off Share my rating on a jar to take its rating back.

Other people see a jar's GLOBAL average and how many people rated it. They don't see your individual ratings. Your photos, reviews, prices, dab history, temperatures and dab ratings are never sent, even when you are signed in.

Bluetooth

Torchless uses Bluetooth only to talk to the rig you pick. It reads the rig's status (temperature, battery, settings) and sends the commands you tap, like heating or changing lights. The rig's data is not sent anywhere else.

Camera and photos

If you add a jar photo, Torchless uses the camera or photo picker only when you tap to do so. Photos stay on your device.

Services that load the app

The app's pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts load from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services receive your device's IP address and basic browser details when the app loads. Their own privacy policies apply: GitHub and Google. On Android, the app opens in Chrome, and Chrome's own settings and policy apply.

Deleting your data

Delete jars inside the app, or remove everything on your phone by clearing the app's storage (uninstall the Android app, or clear site data for torchlessapp.github.io in your browser).

If you signed in, delete your account in the app (JARS, tap your name, Delete account). This removes your sign-in, profile and every shared rating right away. Jar names you added to the shared catalog stay, with no name attached. See Delete your account, which also covers how to ask by email.

Age

Torchless is for adults 21 and older where cannabis use is legal. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the developer email listed on the Torchless page in Google Play.

Torchless is not affiliated with Dr. Dabber.
