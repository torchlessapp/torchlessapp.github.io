TORCHLESS
Heat-up control for your e-rig

Heat, watch and cut your rig from your phone. Works with the Dr. Dabber Switch 2. For adults 21 and over.

  1. Install Bluefy

    Apple's Safari can't talk to Bluetooth devices. Bluefy is a free browser that can.

    Get Bluefy from the App Store
  2. Open Torchless in Bluefy

    Tap the button below. If it doesn't switch over, open Bluefy and type torchless.brickandsignal.com.

    OPEN IN BLUEFY
  3. Link your rig

    Turn the rig on and keep it close. Confirm you're 21+, tap LINK IT and pick your rig from the list.

  4. Go full screen

    Open Bluefy's menu and turn on Full-screen Mode. Bluefy turns it off each time it opens, so Torchless reminds you.

Keep Torchless open during a dab. If you leave it, the rig finishes the dab on its own timer.
