Jar Journal Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Jar Journal is made by Brick & Signal LLC. It is a journal for your concentrate jars: what you paid, how each one tastes, and how you rate it. This policy covers the Jar Journal app for iPhone.

The short version

You can use Jar Journal without an account, and then the only things sent to our server are your searches of the global list and the names you type when adding a jar, so the app can show matches. If you sign in, the ratings, prices per gram and flavor tags you choose to share are stored on our server so they can count toward the global averages. Your dab log and notes always stay on your phone, and so do your jar photos, except a jar's cover photo, which is shared with its rating unless you switch that off. Jar Journal has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell your information.

What stays on your phone

  • Your jars: strain, producer, type, your review, photos (except ones you share), the shop and what you paid.
  • Your dab log: time, how you had it, temperature, size, rating and notes.
  • Your setup and app settings, and that you confirmed you are 21 or older.

These are saved in the app's storage on your device and are never sent to us. If you back up your iPhone, they may be included in your own device backup.

What we store if you sign in

Signing in is optional and uses Sign in with Apple. With an account we store:

  • Your sign-in. Apple gives us an account identifier and an email address, which can be Apple's private relay address if you choose to hide your email.
  • Your profile. A display name, an Instagram handle if you add one, and the date you confirmed you are 21 or older. Your display name and handle can be seen by other signed-in users.
  • Your shared ratings. For each jar with sharing on, its 0 to 10 rating, stored against your account.
  • Your shared prices. For each jar with price sharing on, the price per gram and the jar size. The shop name and the total you paid are not sent.
  • Your flavor tags. The flavor tags you pick for a jar.
  • Photos you share. When a jar's rating is shared, its cover photo is shared with it, unless you turn off Share my photo for that jar. If you share a jar photo, we store the image, which jar it belongs to, and that it came from your account. Before upload the app redraws the image, which removes its location and camera details. Other people see shared photos without your name, and see only how many likes a photo has, not who liked it. Likes you give are stored with your account.
  • Reports you send. If you report a product name or a photo, the report and the reason you picked are stored with your account so a moderator can review it. The person who added it is not told who reported it.
  • Product names you add. When you rate a jar nobody has rated before, its producer, strain and type are added to the shared catalog so others can find it.

Sharing is on by default when you are signed in. You can turn it off for any jar on that jar's page, and what was sent for that jar is removed from the server.

What other people see

Other people see the shared catalog of product names and the global numbers for each product: the average rating, the number of ratings, the average price per gram and the most picked flavors. No name is shown next to any single rating, price or tag. On our server they are stored against your account so that you can change or remove them.

Searching and name suggestions

When you search the global list, or type a strain name while adding a jar, what you type is sent to our server to find matching products. This works with or without an account. We do not save your searches with your account. Our database host keeps standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for a short time to run and secure the service.

Automatic check of new product names

New product names and shared photos are checked by an automated moderation service (Anthropic's Claude) before other people can see them, to keep out things like contact details, sale offers and pictures of people. Only the name, or the photo and the jar's name, are sent for that check, not who added them. A name the check is unsure about is looked at by a person.

Who holds the data

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database host, on servers in the United States. Supabase and Anthropic handle this data only to provide their service to us. We do not share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Camera and photos

If you add a jar photo, Jar Journal uses the camera or photo picker only when you tap to do so. A jar's cover photo is shared with its rating unless you turn off Share my photo for that jar; other photos stay on your device unless you tap to share one. Turning sharing off, or removing a shared photo, deletes it from our server.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: delete jars or dabs inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.
  • On our server: in the app, open your account and choose Delete account. This removes your sign-in, profile, ratings, prices, flavor tags, likes and shared photos right away. Product names you added stay in the shared catalog, no longer linked to you.

If an account is banned for breaking the rules, we keep a scrambled marker of its sign-in, which cannot be turned back into the identifier, so that the ban still holds after the account is deleted.

You can also email us to ask for a copy of what we hold about your account, or to have it deleted.

Age

Jar Journal is for adults 21 and older where cannabis use is legal. It is not meant for anyone younger, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe someone under 21 has made an account, email us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when reviews and comments are added, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
